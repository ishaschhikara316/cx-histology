The cohort-scaling story

Three model snapshots on the same prediction task — 7 cohorts (n=289), 15 cohorts (n=1,454), 21 cohorts. Smaller cohort sets achieve larger pan-cohort ρ partly because cancer-type heterogeneity is doing the work.

SettingMean ρn

Pan-cohort ρ has a strong cancer-type-classification component. As more cohorts are added, the within-biology signal becomes the dominant explanation, and effect sizes shrink toward their unbiased estimate. The 21-cohort number is the most honest estimate of the H&E→CIN biology link.
